Explore Different Music Instruments

Guitar

The guitar is a popular string instrument used in many genres of music. It typically has six strings and can be played acoustically or electrically.

Piano

The piano is a keyboard instrument with a wide range, known for its ability to play both melody and accompaniment. It is central to classical and contemporary music.

Drums

Drums are a percussion instrument used to produce rhythmic sounds. They are often used in various music genres to maintain rhythm and pace.
