Music Classes

Sitari Zonal Institute of Music Education was established in the year 2015 and became a government registered institution on the 10th day of the tenth month of 2022.
Practical and theoretical teaching related to the subject of music is carried out in the Sitari Regional Institute of Music Education.
Singing and instrumental music are taught without any age limit and it is allowed to appear for all the current music exams in Sri Lanka through the Sitari Zone Music Institute.
There is also the possibility to face the music scholar examination affiliated to Bhatkhanda University in India through Sitari Zoning Institute of Music.

Musical Instruments

We provide a diverse range of musical instruments for purchase or rental. Whether you're looking for guitars, pianos, or drums, we have quality instruments for musicians of all skill levels.
